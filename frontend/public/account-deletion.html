Delete your account and data

You can delete your account from the website without installing the Android app.

  1. Sign in to your account using your email and current password.
  2. Open Security in the navigation menu and select Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password, type DELETE, and confirm the deletion.

In Android, open Account in the navigation bar and select Delete account and data. An internet connection is required.

Deletion is permanent. It removes your account and its associated financial records from the application and revokes your sessions. Deleting from Android also clears the local account data on that device, including pending changes. Other signed-in devices clear their local account data when they next connect and detect the revoked session.

If you want a copy of your data first, download your privacy export from the website's Security page before deleting your account.
